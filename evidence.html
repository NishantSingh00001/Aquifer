FORECAST & SCENARIO METHOD

What the model actually does.

Every value can be recomputed from these simple steps. No paid API, hidden model, or remote inference is involved.

01

Choose an observed baseline

Use a selected site’s latest non-missing observation from the 2021–22 sample, or a row imported by the user.

02

Estimate a bounded seasonal slope

Estimate a simple slope across the available dated values, then cap it to ±0.5 metres per year. Sparse rounds make this a weak trend signal.

03

Apply explicit sensitivity factors

Illustrative coefficients: each 10-point rainfall shift changes depth by 0.10 m/year; each +10% pumping adds 0.12 m/year; each +10% recharge effort subtracts 0.06 m/year.

04

Show a range, not certainty

The band adds ±0.15 m per projected year around the central path. It is a visual assumption range, not a statistical confidence interval.

LIMITS THAT MATTER

Read before acting.

01Historical, not live

Bundled readings are from 2021–22 and are not current monitoring.

02Small, incomplete sample

Only 15 named records and three seasonal rounds are bundled; blanks are preserved.

03No surveyed coordinates

Map markers are schematic. They cannot be used for field navigation or site boundaries.

04Not calibrated hydrology

Scenario coefficients are transparent demonstration assumptions, not validated recharge or pumping response.

05No allocation advice

This prototype cannot decide household, farm, or industrial water entitlements.

06Human review required

Check the source report and local experts before using any value in planning.
